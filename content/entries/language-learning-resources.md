## Language learning resources

### German

- [German in 3 months](https://www.amazon.com/German-Months-Free-Audio-Understanding/dp/0744051614): textbook teaching German from scratch (I actually like the older editions of this book from Hugo better)
- [Dino lernt Deutsch](https://books.learnoutlive.com/category/dino-lernt-deutsch/): book series describing the misadventures of an international student in Germany, increasing in difficulty level
- [Tagesschau in einfacher Sprache](https://www.tagesschau.de/tagesschau_in_einfacher_sprache): daily news explained in simplified German

### Russian

- [The New Penguin Russian Course](https://www.amazon.com/New-Penguin-Russian-Course-Beginners/dp/0140120416): curriculum building up Russian knowledge from zero, very succinct and well-structured
- [Russian with Max](https://www.russianwithmax.com/): comprehensible input from A2-B2 level about interesting topics, also on Spotify
- [Удивительное истории: 116 текстов для чтения, изучения и развлечения](https://books.google.de/books/about/Удивительные_истории.html?id=5uoKAQAAMAAJ&redir_esc=y): Russian stories, often from famous authors, rewritten to use simple language and with uncommon vocab in the margins
- Lernen Sie Russland kennen! (Valeria M. Nechaeva): book with authentic Russian readings and vocab definitions in the footnotes exploring Russia after the end of the USSR (but you have to know German) 

### Chinese

- [Hanzi Writer Demo](https://hanziwriter.org/demo.html): visualize correct stroke order of Chinese characters online
- [Chinese Grammar Wiki](https://www.allsetlearning.com): explanations of Chinese grammar topics, sorted by A/B/C or by HSK levels
- [Lazy Chinese](https://www.lazychinese.com): comprehensible input in Mandarin, sorted by proficiency level and with interactive transcripts (both free and premium content)
- [一千字說華語中英文版](https://www.huayuworld.org/upload/eBookstore/eBook/國別話教材(001-204)/013.一千字說華語中英文版/index.html): open Mandarin textbook from Taiwan
- [Chinese in Levels](https://chineseinlevels.com): short daily news articles in Simplified Chinese, separated by difficulty level
- [A Frequency Dictionary of Mandarin Chinese](https://www.routledge.com/A-Frequency-Dictionary-of-Mandarin-Chinese-Core-Vocabulary-for-Learners/Xiao/p/book/9780415455862) from Routledge
- [The Journey to the West in Easy Chinese](https://www.amazon.com/Journey-West-Easy-Chinese/dp/1959043102): the classical novel, rewritten to use a more limited vocabulary and set of characters
- [PunMemeCN](https://github.com/Zhijun-Xu/PunMemeCN): a GitHub repo of Chinese memes, together with structured data providing an explanation of each pun meme and example chat contexts in which it would be used
